01 — About Me

Design that sits at the intersection of strategy and craft.

TR

Tony Romany

Graphic Designer, Cairo

I picked up a mouse and a pirated copy of Photoshop at fourteen and never really put it down. What started as fixing friends' Facebook covers turned into a career built on one belief: good design should feel inevitable, not decorated.

Today I work independently with founders, small studios and growing brands on identity, packaging and print — staying hands-on with every project instead of handing it off. Every brief starts with the same question: what does this brand need to say, and what's the simplest way to say it well?

My Approach

Design decisions should be defensible — every color, typeface and layout choice answers back to the brief.

My Focus

Helping smaller brands look and feel as considered as the big ones, without losing what makes them distinct.
